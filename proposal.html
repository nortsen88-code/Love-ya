tap the box
— written just for you —
I found forever in your laugh,
in quiet mornings, your soft gaze —
no map could chart what you have made
of all my ordinary days.
You are the reason seasons glow,
the voice that turns a house to home.
Wherever life decides to go,
I never want to go alone.
So here I am, on bended heart,
with every word I mean to say —
will you be my greatest art,
and choose me, love, from this day?
Will you marry me? 💍 ❤️
